## 03 · 编织花结

An original compact four-lobed woven knot symbol, four broad curved blue ribbons threading over and under each other around a crisp diamond-shaped transparent negative-space center. Mathematical and poetic, like folded woven paper. Single cobalt-blue family with restrained tonal shading to explain overlaps. Flat vector-like edges, assertive silhouette, only four lobes. Avoid the OpenAI logo, avoid sixfold symmetry, avoid generic AI sparkles.

## 04 · 书页扇面

A beautiful abstract fan of three thick folded archival pages, forming a compact wing-like symbol and a subtle W through the negative spaces. Unequal page lengths create a deliberate upward rhythm. Terracotta-orange solid surfaces, controlled soft 3D paper depth. Editorial and calm, not a literal folder or book illustration. Three clean planes only, sculptural silhouette.

## 05 · 黑白榫结
File: 05.png

A highly original graphic symbol formed by two interlocking thick charcoal-black angular strokes, inspired by traditional joinery and textile weaving. Compact near-square overall silhouette with a bold offset transparent central cut-out and precisely cut diagonal joints. Elegant intelligent negative space. Flat monochrome vector-like execution, no gradients, no texture. Recognizable at 24 pixels, not a generic maze, not a QR code, not a letter in a box.
